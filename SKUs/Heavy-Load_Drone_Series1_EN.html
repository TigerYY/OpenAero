🏢 About Us

Guangzhou TRIRM Electric Equipment Co., Ltd. is dedicated to the research and development of next-generation industrial Internet and intelligent equipment technologies. We provide complete solutions for industrial scenarios, ranging from data acquisition and edge computing to intelligent control. Our product portfolio includes industrial-grade IoT gateways (4G/5G), smart distribution gateways, intelligent sensors, and universal protocol interconnection platforms. We also specialize in drone R&D, testing, production, and custom intelligent equipment development, forming a comprehensive capability system of "integrated software and hardware + industry applications".

OpenAero (Website: openaero.cn) is a professional brand under TRIRM, focusing on the drone sector. OpenAero adheres to the philosophy of "Open Innovation, Technology Leadership" and specializes in the research, development, and application of industrial-grade drone systems. We provide multi-scenario solutions including agricultural drones, heavy-load transport drones, and inspection drones. Leveraging open-source flight control technology and independent R&D capabilities, OpenAero delivers cost-effective and highly reliable drone products with customization support, empowering digital transformation in smart agriculture, logistics, infrastructure inspection, and other industries.
